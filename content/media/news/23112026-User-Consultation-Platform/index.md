Registration is now open for the User Consultation Platform (UCP) 2026, organised by the European Union Agency for the Space Programme (EUSPA). The event will take place on 26–27 November 2026 in Prague, Czech Republic, with an option to participate online.

The UCP provides a working forum for businesses, public authorities, service providers, start-ups and research organisations to engage directly with EUSPA, share their experiences and communicate the challenges and opportunities within their respective sectors.

The first day will feature parallel consultation sessions focusing on different user communities and market segments. Discussions will explore how EU Space capabilities can address practical needs across sectors including agriculture, aviation and drones, fisheries and aquaculture, infrastructure, energy and raw materials, urban development, tourism, health, and environmental monitoring.

On the second day, the UCP Plenary will bring together insights from the consultation sessions, allowing participants and industry representatives to discuss findings and identify concrete follow-up actions to strengthen the relevance and uptake of EU Space services.

Beyond the consultations, participants will have opportunities to explore funding and business opportunities, attend practical demonstrations, engage with Horizon Europe and CASSINI activities, and connect with stakeholders from across the European space ecosystem.

For organisations working at the intersection of Earth Observation, IoT, agriculture, environmental monitoring and digital innovation, the platform offers an opportunity to engage with the wider EU Space community and explore how space-based services can respond to real-world user needs.

Waziup encourages innovators, researchers and organisations interested in applying space technologies to practical challenges to explore this opportunity and contribute to the discussions.

Event details:
- Dates: 26–27 November 2026
- Location: Prague, Czech Republic, and online
- Organiser: European Union Agency for the Space Programme (EUSPA)
