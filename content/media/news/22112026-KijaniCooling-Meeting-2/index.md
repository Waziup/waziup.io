The KijaniCooling consortium held its 3rd Annual Project Meeting from 3–5 November 2026 in Italy, bringing together project partners to review progress, exchange technical knowledge and discuss the next steps in developing sustainable organic waste management and cooling solutions for Africa.

The three-day meeting took place at the ENEA Research Centre of Trisaia in Rotondella and Casa Cava in Matera, providing an opportunity for partners to share updates across the project's work packages and engage in technical discussions on the development and implementation of the KijaniCooling solution.

The first day featured institutional presentations and an introduction to the ENEA Research Centre's activities, followed by a visit to its anaerobic digestion experimental site. Partners also held technical discussions on the anaerobic digestion system and presented progress across the project's work packages.

On the second day, partners continued with work package presentations and discussions, followed by a guided visit to Matera's city centre. These sessions provided an opportunity to review ongoing activities, exchange perspectives and strengthen coordination across the consortium.

The final day focused on technical visits and demonstrations at the ENEA Research Centre, including its research facilities and platforms. Participants also took part in a virtual visit to Politecnico di Milano laboratories and a chiller demonstration. Further technical discussions addressed the chiller and cooling room systems, alongside a workshop on the project's financial sustainability.

The meeting provided an important platform for consortium partners to assess technical progress, share knowledge and coordinate activities towards the demonstration and validation of the KijaniCooling solution.

Learn more about the project here: https://www.kijanicooling.eu/

Through continued collaboration between research institutions, technology developers and implementation partners, KijaniCooling remains committed to transforming African organic waste into green energy for cooling, while advancing sustainable waste management and circular economy solutions.
