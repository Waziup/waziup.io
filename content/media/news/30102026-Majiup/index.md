What if managing water could be simpler, smarter and more efficient? Meet MajiUp, an emerging smart water technology company rethinking how water is monitored, distributed and managed through digital innovation.

MajiUp is developing connected water solutions designed for water kiosk operators, property managers, utilities and institutions. By moving beyond manual processes, the company aims to make everyday water operations more efficient, transparent and data-driven.

Through the integration of Internet of Things (IoT), digital payments, remote monitoring and data analytics, MajiUp is helping water service providers improve operational visibility, strengthen accountability and make more informed decisions.

Its approach supports smarter water distribution and sustainable water management while contributing to Sustainable Development Goal 6 (SDG 6): Clean Water and Sanitation.

By bringing technology closer to everyday water operations, MajiUp is working towards a future where every drop can be better monitored, managed and accounted for.
